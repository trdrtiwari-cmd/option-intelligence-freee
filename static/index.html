    Paper Trading — Option Premium (live)

    You buy the selected option at its live premium. Initial stop-loss, trailing stop, target and profit/loss are all measured on the option premium and refreshed from the option chain about every 10 seconds. The trailing stop follows the highest premium the app has seen (stop = peak − trail, never below your initial stop-loss) and can only move up. Stops and targets can only react to the prices the app actually sees, so a fast spike between two refreshes can be missed. Nothing here places a real order.
